# OE Number Policy

Purpose: define how CertiSpares should use OE numbers, part numbers, and cross references safely.

## Core Rule

OE numbers, part numbers, and cross references are matching inputs.

They are not automatic fitment conclusions.

Use them to identify an inquiry, start technical discussion, compare references, and prepare supplier checks. Do not use them to imply verified compatibility unless the required confirmation has happened.

## Allowed Uses

Allowed:

- "Send OE number or part number for matching discussion."
- "OE references help identify the requested part family."
- "Cross references may help compare supplier options."
- "Final scope depends on OE/VIN/model/spec confirmation."
- "Photos and dimensions are recommended when OE references are uncertain."

## Disallowed Uses

Do not write:

- "This OE number fits all..."
- "Guaranteed replacement for..."
- "Official OE replacement..."
- "100% compatible with..."
- "Direct fit for every model..."
- "Certified genuine part..." unless documented

Do not create pages that exist only to capture OE numbers with thin or misleading content.

## Required Context

When OE or part numbers appear on a page, the page should also explain at least one of:

- what additional details are needed
- why OE references may vary
- what matching checks CertiSpares performs
- what photos/specs help reduce mismatch risk
- that final scope requires confirmation

## Cross Reference Language

Safe wording:

- "used as cross-reference input"
- "can support supplier comparison"
- "requires confirmation against vehicle details and visible specifications"
- "may not be sufficient by itself"

Unsafe wording:

- "interchangeable with all listed numbers"
- "identical to genuine part"
- "universal replacement"
- "official equivalent"

## Brand Name Use

Brand names can be used to identify buyer inquiry context.

Do not use brand names to imply:

- authorization
- official dealership
- official service network participation
- guaranteed genuine parts supply
- OEM endorsement

## Recommended Disclaimer

Use this where OE, part number, vehicle model, or cross-reference content appears:

> Brand names, OE numbers, vehicle models, and cross references on this site are used for inquiry identification and matching discussion only. Final fitment and quotation scope must be confirmed by OE reference, VIN, model/year/market data, dimensions, connector type, and other applicable technical specifications. CertiSpares is an independent sourcing and RFQ support service unless explicit authorization is stated.

## Page-Type Rules

Brand pages:

- may mention common RFQ references
- should ask for OE/VIN/model/photos
- should not publish exact-fitment lists

System pages:

- may explain how OE references support matching
- should focus on system-specific matching fields
- should not imply all parts in the system are interchangeable

Product-family pages:

- may discuss OE/part numbers as inquiry inputs
- should explain variables and mismatch risks
- should avoid exact compatibility claims

RFQ page:

- should collect OE/part number
- should make clear that additional data may be needed

## Acceptance Criteria

OE usage is safe if:

- the number is framed as an input
- final fitment remains subject to confirmation
- authorization is not implied
- supporting fields are requested
- the page provides useful context beyond the number itself
