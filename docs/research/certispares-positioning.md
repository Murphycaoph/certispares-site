# CertiSpares Positioning

Purpose: define what CertiSpares is, what it is not, and how every page should frame the business.

## Core Definition

CertiSpares is an RFQ-first commercial vehicle aftermarket sourcing site.

It helps overseas buyers prepare structured inquiries, compare supplier options, control matching risk, coordinate QC, handle documents, and consolidate export shipments.

CertiSpares should be presented as a sourcing workflow and RFQ support service, not as an online catalogue or direct retail store.

## Audience

Primary buyers:

- importers
- distributors
- wholesalers
- fleet maintenance buyers
- mixed-SKU commercial vehicle parts buyers

Typical buyer problem:

- they need several commercial vehicle parts from China
- they may know the brand, part system, OE number, photos, or dimensions
- they need supplier comparison, matching review, packaging coordination, and shipment support
- they do not need a fake public SKU database

## Positioning Pillars

Use these themes consistently:

- RFQ-first sourcing
- commercial vehicle aftermarket focus
- independent sourcing partner
- multi-supplier consolidation
- technical matching before quotation
- QC and document coordination
- heavy-duty system depth
- Northern China supplier relevance where appropriate

## What CertiSpares Is Not

Do not describe the site as:

- a full product catalogue
- a public SKU database
- an official OEM dealer
- an authorized service network
- a factory
- a marketplace with live stock
- a price comparison website with guaranteed prices
- a generic corporate brochure

If authorization exists for any brand or product line, it must be explicitly documented before being claimed.

## Site-Wide Messaging Rules

Preferred language:

- inquiry-based sourcing
- RFQ support
- technical matching discussion
- supplier comparison
- quotation consolidation
- export coordination
- commercial vehicle aftermarket parts
- match-critical details

Avoid:

- buy now
- official replacement
- guaranteed fit
- fits all models
- in stock unless verified
- cheapest supplier
- authorized dealer unless verified
- OEM parts supplier unless the meaning is carefully qualified

## Homepage Test

Within 10 seconds, a visitor should understand:

- CertiSpares is for RFQ-based sourcing
- it is not a catalogue
- the buyer should send OE/VIN/model/spec/photos/quantity/destination where possible
- CertiSpares helps organize matching, sourcing, QC, documents, and shipment

## Page-Level Positioning

Brand pages should say:

- "Start an RFQ by known truck brand or platform."
- "Send OE number, VIN/model, photos, list, quantity, and destination for matching discussion."

System pages should say:

- "Start an RFQ by known part system."
- "Send dimensions, model/VIN, system-specific specs, photos, quantity, and destination."

Product-family pages should say:

- "Use this page to identify the variables and information needed for this part family."
- "Final matching depends on OE/VIN/model/spec confirmation."

RFQ pages should say:

- "Submit a structured inquiry with enough information for matching and supplier comparison."

## Acceptance Criteria

A page follows this positioning if:

- it makes RFQ-first behavior clear
- it avoids catalogue, stock, and exact-fitment overclaims
- it points the user toward a qualified RFQ
- it explains what information the buyer should provide
- it reinforces CertiSpares as independent sourcing support
