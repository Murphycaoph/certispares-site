# CertiSpares Blog Clusters

Purpose: define how blog content should support SEO landing pages and RFQ conversion.

## Blog Role

The blog is an explanation layer for CertiSpares landing pages.

It should not operate as an independent media site. Its job is to educate buyers, clarify sourcing risks, explain product variables, and move users toward brand pages, system pages, product-family pages, sourcing pages, and RFQ.

## Existing Content Strength

The site already has a meaningful blog base across:

- Product Insight
- Sourcing Knowledge
- China Supply Chain
- Strategy & Logistics

Many existing articles already support commercial vehicle parts, sourcing risk, supplier comparison, QC, industrial clusters, and logistics. Future work should organize and link this content better before simply adding volume.

## Cluster Types

| Cluster | Role | Primary link targets |
|---|---|---|
| Product Insight | Explain parts, failure modes, variables, and RFQ inputs | System pages, product-family pages, RFQ |
| Sourcing Knowledge | Explain supplier comparison, quotation review, QC, sourcing risk | Sourcing, Capabilities, How It Works, RFQ |
| China Supply Chain | Explain regional supply chain context and cluster logic | Capabilities, Sourcing, selected system pages |
| Strategy & Logistics | Explain consolidation, shipping, documents, landed cost | Sourcing, Capabilities, RFQ |
| Brand/Platform Guide | Support real brand RFQ depth | Brand page, related system pages, RFQ |

## Product Insight Rules

Product Insight articles should:

- explain what the part is
- explain how it fails or varies
- explain buyer-side specs
- name common mismatch risks
- link to a system page
- link to a product-family page when available
- link to RFQ

Do not write product articles that end without a clear landing-page or RFQ destination.

## Sourcing Knowledge Rules

Sourcing Knowledge articles should:

- explain supplier selection
- explain quotation comparison
- explain quality control
- explain documents and shipment coordination
- link to Sourcing, Capabilities, How It Works, or RFQ

Avoid generic "how to buy from China" content unless it clearly supports CertiSpares RFQ workflows.

## China Supply Chain Rules

China Supply Chain articles should:

- explain why regional supplier networks matter
- connect to North China or heavy-duty system relevance where true
- support trust in sourcing process
- avoid unsupported superiority claims

## Brand Guide Rules

Only create brand/platform guides when CertiSpares has enough RFQ depth or strategic need for that brand.

Brand guides should:

- support a specific brand page
- explain what buyers should prepare
- link to relevant systems
- avoid acting like an official brand catalogue

## Kill List

Avoid or deprioritize articles that:

- cannot link naturally to brand, system, product-family, sourcing, capabilities, or RFQ pages
- chase broad traffic without RFQ intent
- copy supplier catalogue copy
- imply exact fitment
- publish unsupported brand claims
- focus on consumer car topics outside CertiSpares scope
- duplicate existing articles without a clearer landing-page role

## Acceptance Criteria

A blog article is useful if:

- it supports at least one commercial page
- it clarifies a real buyer decision or matching risk
- it has meaningful internal links
- it avoids catalogue and fitment overclaims
- it moves readers toward qualified RFQ behavior
